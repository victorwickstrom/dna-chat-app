Step 1

Get Your DNA File

Don't worry – it's easy! Here's how to download your DNA file from each provider

23andMe

  • 1 Log in to your 23andMe account at 23andme.com
  • 2 Click on your name in the top right corner
  • 3 Select "Settings" from the dropdown menu
  • 4 Scroll down to "23andMe Data" section
  • 5 Click "View" next to "Download Raw Data"
  • 6 Enter your password and click "Download"
Go to 23andMe Download

AncestryDNA

  • 1 Log in to your Ancestry account at ancestry.com
  • 2 Click on "DNA" in the top navigation
  • 3 Click "Settings" in the DNA menu
  • 4 Scroll to "Download Raw DNA Data"
  • 5 Click "Download" and confirm your password
  • 6 Check your email for the download link
Go to Ancestry Settings

MyHeritage

  • 1 Log in to your MyHeritage account
  • 2 Click on "DNA" in the top menu
  • 3 Select "Manage DNA kits"
  • 4 Click on the three dots (⋮) next to your kit
  • 5 Select "Download raw DNA data"
  • 6 Confirm and download the ZIP file
Go to MyHeritage DNA

FamilyTreeDNA

  • 1 Log in to your FTDNA account
  • 2 Navigate to "myDNA" section
  • 3 Click on "Download Raw Data"
  • 4 Select "Concatenated" format
  • 5 Click Download and save the file
Go to FTDNA

LivingDNA

  • 1 Log in to your LivingDNA account
  • 2 Go to "My Account" settings
  • 3 Find "Raw DNA data" section
  • 4 Click "Download" button
  • 5 Save the downloaded file
Go to LivingDNA

Other Providers

  • 1 Log in to your DNA testing provider
  • 2 Look for "Settings" or "Account"
  • 3 Search for "Raw Data" or "Download"
  • 4 Download as TXT or CSV format
  • 5 VCF files are also supported

Most providers offer raw data export in their account settings.
